Common questions

How do I see the tutorial again?

Tap How to play? in the main menu, or open your profile and choose Replay tutorial. Tap the screen during the opening ramp to skip straight to the road.

What do ROCKET MODE and the item boxes do?

Tricks, near misses and cars you jump over fill the ROCKET bar at the top. When it's full you get 8 seconds of ROCKET MODE: faster, invincible and worth triple points. Item boxes on the road refill your AIR or give you a HEAT SHIELD straight away, or put SHOCKWAVE or LUDICROUS in the ITEM slot above BOOST — tap it when you need it.

What is AIR used for?

Almost everything: jumping, front flips, lane changes, slams, flying and BOOST each cost a little AIR. It refills slowly while you drive on the road, and quickly with AIR cells and AIR CANs. Below 20% the bar turns red; with no AIR a front flip won't start and lane changes are slower.

How do I change the language, sound or vibration?

Tap the profile button at the top left of the main menu. Switch between System, Türkçe and English, turn sound on or off, set engine and effects volume separately, or turn vibration off. The speaker button at the top right mutes everything in one tap.

I can't hear anything

Check that Sound on is enabled in your profile and both volume sliders are above zero. Roadastar follows your iPhone's silent switch — turn silent mode off and raise the volume.

Can I change my pilot name?

Yes. Open your profile, edit the name and tap Save. Letters, digits and underscores, 3 to 14 characters. The number after your name stays the same.

Where is my best score kept?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account, so scores don't move to another device, and deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

What are R coins, the garage and Revive?

Achievements and missions pay out R coins. In the Garage you spend them on paints (1,000 R each; Pearl White is free) and on Revive (100 R): after a crash you have 5 seconds to use it, the road is cleared and you keep your score and distance, once per run. There is nothing to buy with real money.

How does the Game Center ranking work?

It's optional and off by default. Turn it on in Profile: iOS signs you in to Game Center, and after each run your best score is submitted to the leaderboard. Ranking in the main menu opens the board. Switch it off and nothing is sent.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on the device and works in airplane mode. The only online feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard, and it stays off until you turn it on.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The game is designed for portrait.

Report a problem

Crash, stutter or something that looks wrong? Email what happened and what you expected. Roadastar has no analytics and sends no crash reports — your message is the only way a bug gets found.

Gameplay

  • Every run starts upside down on a towering loop ramp, then GO
  • Lane changes, jumps, flying on the AIR tank and slam landings
  • Score, streak and mode bars with a live event feed; pause any time
  • A ridiculous excuse for every run, 12 in all

Tricks and combo

  • Front flips and barrel rolls mid-air, STUNT LANDING for a clean touchdown
  • CLEAR, MULTI JUMP and NEAR MISS bonuses
  • Chain skills to push the streak multiplier up to ×50
  • BOOST for extra speed and bonus points

ROCKET and STAR MODE

  • Stunts fill the ROCKET bar: 8 seconds of ROCKET MODE, faster, invincible and ×3 points
  • Golden star: 8 seconds of STAR MODE that sends cars flying

Pickups and items

  • AIR cells and AIR CAN refills above the road
  • Item boxes: OVERPRESSURE, HEAT SHIELD, SHOCKWAVE and LUDICROUS
  • An ITEM slot above BOOST for the item you save for later

Profile and settings

  • Interactive tutorial on the first run, replayable any time
  • Your pilot name and best score, kept on your device
  • English and Turkish, separate engine and effect volume, haptics on or off

Regions

  • Four worlds, 3 km each: rocket test site, sunset highway, salt flats and neon city
  • The sky changes from golden afternoon to sunset, a starlit night and neon dusk
  • Rockets lift off beside the road; the city runs on a viaduct high above the ground

Car

  • A new white-and-blue roadster with an open cockpit and rear wing
  • Ten thrusters: lift jets for jumps and flying, rear jets for BOOST, side jets for lane changes
  • In STAR MODE the car cycles through rainbow colours

Road

  • The road widens as you go: a fourth lane at 2.5 km, a fifth at 5 km
  • Overpasses: fly under one with inches to spare for UNDERPASS, but mind the deck
  • Green direction signs over the road

Traffic

  • Speed climbs with distance, up to 100 m/s
  • Traffic gets denser the further you drive, but there is always a way through
  • SUVs, limos, city and school buses, semi and long semi trucks join as your score grows
  • Fly over a semi for SEMI CLEARED
  • Lane-changers that signal, parked rows with hazard lights, roadworks that close a lane

Handling

  • Jumps are 2.5 m high and get snappier as you speed up
  • Hold to fly up to a 6.2 m ceiling; a front flip gives you a lift
  • Swipe down mid-trick to land fast and still score the trick
  • Swipe up works like a tap: jump on the road, front flip in the air
  • Hold BOOST during a power mode for hyper boost

AIR

  • Every move costs AIR: jump 20 L, front flip 40 L, lane change 3 L, slam 20 L, flying and BOOST per second
  • AIR refills slowly on the road; AIR cells glow white and arc over the cars you jump
  • Below 20% the bar turns red; with no AIR, front flips won't start and lane changes are slower
  • 12-segment AIR bar: red when low, mode colours during ROCKET, STAR MODE and LUDICROUS
  • During ROCKET, STAR MODE and LUDICROUS, AIR is unlimited and nearby AIR cells are pulled to the car
