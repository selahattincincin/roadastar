Roadastar

Drop off the ramp. Dodge the traffic. Fly over it.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • Offline
  • No account needed

Roadastar is a one-thumb arcade highway runner. Launch upside down off a loop ramp, swipe between lanes, jump and fly over cars, and chain flips and barrel rolls into a streak. Fill the ROCKET bar for eight invincible seconds, grab the golden star or crack open an item box, and send traffic flying. The further you go, the faster it gets: buses, semi trucks, roadworks and bridges join the traffic, and the highway opens up lane by lane from three to five. Every 3 km the world changes, from a rocket test site to sunset palms, starlit salt flats and a neon city. It runs entirely on your iPhone — no account, no server of ours, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on the optional Game Center leaderboard.

Common questions

How do I see the tutorial again?

Tap How to play? in the main menu, or open your profile and choose Replay tutorial. Tap the screen during the opening ramp to skip straight to the road.

What do ROCKET MODE and the item boxes do?

Tricks, near misses and cars you jump over fill the ROCKET bar at the top. When it's full you get 8 seconds of ROCKET MODE: faster, invincible and worth triple points. Item boxes on the road refill your AIR or give you a HEAT SHIELD straight away, or put SHOCKWAVE or LUDICROUS in the ITEM slot above BOOST — tap it when you need it.

What is AIR used for?

Almost everything: jumping, front flips, lane changes, slams, flying and BOOST each cost a little AIR. It refills slowly while you drive on the road, and quickly with AIR cells and AIR CANs. Below 20% the bar turns red; with no AIR a front flip won't start and lane changes are slower.

How do I change the language, sound or vibration?

Tap the profile button at the top left of the main menu. Switch between System, Türkçe and English, turn sound on or off, set engine and effects volume separately, or turn vibration off. The speaker button at the top right mutes everything in one tap.

I can't hear anything

Check that Sound on is enabled in your profile and both volume sliders are above zero. Roadastar follows your iPhone's silent switch — turn silent mode off and raise the volume.

Can I change my pilot name?

Yes. Open your profile, edit the name and tap Save. Letters, digits and underscores, 3 to 14 characters. The number after your name stays the same.

Where is my best score kept?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account, so scores don't move to another device, and deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

What are R coins, the garage and Revive?

Achievements and missions pay out R coins. In the Garage you spend them on paints (1,000 R each; Pearl White is free) and on Revive (100 R): after a crash you have 5 seconds to use it, the road is cleared and you keep your score and distance, once per run. There is nothing to buy with real money.

How does the Game Center ranking work?

It's optional and off by default. Turn it on in Profile: iOS signs you in to Game Center, and after each run your best score is submitted to the leaderboard. Ranking in the main menu opens the board. Switch it off and nothing is sent.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on the device and works in airplane mode. The only online feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard, and it stays off until you turn it on.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The game is designed for portrait.
