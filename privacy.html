Privacy

  • No servers of ours
  • Ads only if you choose
  • No account needed
  • You control tracking

Roadastar is a game you can play offline. We run no servers and receive no personal data. The online extras are optional: Apple's Game Center leaderboard, a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account and — from version 1.3 — an ad from Google AdMob that appears only when you choose to watch one to continue a run. This page explains exactly what the app keeps, what Google may collect for those ads and how you stay in control.

Who is responsible

Roadastar is made and published by an independent developer, shown as the seller on the App Store page. Questions about this policy: selahattincincin@gmail.com.

Everything below is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. From version 1.1, your game progress (rows marked “Your device and your iCloud”) is also copied to your own iCloud account so it survives a new iPhone or reinstalling the app. Settings and reminder choices stay on the device.

What we don't do

  • We run no servers and receive no data from the app. Apart from the optional extras on this page (Game Center, iCloud and optional ads), the game makes no network connections.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own. The only third-party code is Google's ads SDK, used only for the optional ads described below.
  • There is no account or sign-in of our own. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt.
  • The app doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or health data. (For ads, Google may estimate an approximate region from your IP address.)
  • We don't sell data — we never receive any.

Game Center (optional)

The Ranking leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center and is off by default. If you turn it on in Profile, the app asks iOS to sign you in to Game Center, and after each run your best score is submitted to the leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy; we receive nothing.

Turning the switch off stops the submissions. Scores already on the leaderboard are managed through Game Center in the iOS Settings app.

iCloud (from version 1.1)

If you are signed in to iCloud, Roadastar keeps a copy of your progress — pilot name, best score, coins, paints, items, achievements and quests — in iCloud key-value storage that belongs to your Apple account. It is used only to bring your progress back on another device or after reinstalling.

This copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud. We have no access to it and no server of ours is involved. It stops if you sign out of iCloud or turn off iCloud for Roadastar in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Reminders (optional, from version 1.1)

If you allow notifications, Roadastar schedules at most one reminder a day — never at night — on your iPhone: your check-in streak, rewards waiting to be claimed or new daily quests. They are created on the device by iOS; no push service is used and nothing is sent anywhere. Turn them off in Profile › Reminders or in iOS Settings.

Ads (optional, from version 1.3)

Roadastar shows an ad only when you choose to: after a crash, “Watch ad to continue” lets you carry on from where you crashed, once per run. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. If no ad is available (for example offline), the option simply doesn't appear.

These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect a device identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, which ads you saw and how you interacted with them, and diagnostic data. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (rating G).

On iOS you decide in the system prompt (App Tracking Transparency) whether ads may be personalised; you can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it in Profile › Ads › Ad privacy options. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Roadastar is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Deleting your data

  • Change your pilot name in the profile screen; the old name is replaced.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.
  • Your iCloud copy is replaced as soon as you play on any device; signing out of iCloud or turning off iCloud for Roadastar stops it.

Children

Roadastar collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The optional ads are limited to content suitable for all ages, and only appear when the player chooses to watch one.

Your rights

No data about you reaches us, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf; everything the app stores stays under your control on your device and in your own iCloud. For data Google processes for the optional ads, see Google's privacy policy and your choices in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking (and Profile › Ads in the EEA and UK). Questions: selahattincincin@gmail.com.
