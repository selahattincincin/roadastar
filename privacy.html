Privacy

Roadastar · Last updated 25 October 2026

  • No data collected
  • No servers of ours
  • No ads or tracking
  • No account needed

Roadastar is an offline game. It does not collect, send or share any personal data. The only online feature is Apple's optional Game Center leaderboard, which is off until you turn it on. This page explains exactly what the app keeps on your iPhone and why.

Who is responsible

Roadastar is made and published by an independent developer, shown as the seller on the App Store page. Questions about this policy: selahattincincin@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Everything below is saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it leaves the device.

Like any app's data, this may be included in the device backup that iOS makes to your own iCloud or computer. The developer cannot access those backups.

What we don't do

  • We run no servers and receive no data from the app. The game itself makes no network connections; the only online feature is the optional Game Center leaderboard described below.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code.
  • There is no account or sign-in of our own, and no tracking across apps and websites.
  • The app doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or health data.
  • We don't sell or share data — we never receive any.

Game Center (optional)

The Ranking leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center and is off by default. If you turn it on in Profile, the app asks iOS to sign you in to Game Center, and after each run your best score is submitted to the leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy; we receive nothing.

Turning the switch off stops the submissions. Scores already on the leaderboard are managed through Game Center in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Roadastar is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Deleting your data

  • Change your pilot name in the profile screen; the old name is replaced.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.

Children

Roadastar collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows no ads.

Your rights

Because no data about you reaches us, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. Everything the app stores stays under your control on your device. Questions: selahattincincin@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
